Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 07, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MetWet. Your privacy is critical to us. This policy explains how we handle data within the MetWet application.

2. Data Collection

MetWet is designed as an offline utility. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All calculations are performed locally on your device.

3. Permissions

The app may request storage permissions only if required to export your calculation results. No personal media or files are accessed.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not use third-party tracking or analytics services that harvest your data. Your usage remains private and on your device.

5. Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data leakage from our servers. We prioritize the security and integrity of the local application.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our official support channels.
